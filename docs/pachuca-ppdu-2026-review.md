# PPDU Pachuca–Mineral de la Reforma 2026 — activation gate

## Current decision

The PPDU 2026 remains **PENDING_SCOPE_REVIEW** in EVIDEN. No parcel-level zoning, density, height, COS/CUS, setback, or other PPDU-derived parameter may activate a productive rule until the legal activation conditions below are verified.

## Evidence reviewed

- Official publication: Periódico Oficial del Estado de Hidalgo, Alcance 0 No. 34, 25 August 2026, 514 pages.
- State regulatory framework: Reglamento de la Ley de Asentamientos Humanos, Desarrollo Urbano y Ordenamiento Territorial del Estado de Hidalgo, published 18 May 2015.
- Article 23 of the 2015 Regulation requires approved municipal, population-center, and partial development programs to be sent for publication in the Periódico Oficial and inscription in the Registro Público de la Propiedad y del Comercio.
- Article 25 establishes that, from the date of inscription in the Registro Público, the municipal authority, with prior agreement of the Secretaría, may issue licenses or authorizations for urban actions covered by the program.

## Interpretation for EVIDEN

Official publication is necessary evidence that the instrument exists, but it is not sufficient evidence for EVIDEN to treat every PPDU-derived parcel rule as operational. The registry must separately verify the registration/effective status and the territorial/legal scope before activating machine-readable rules.

This is a product safety rule, not a legal conclusion that the PPDU is invalid or ineffective. EVIDEN should report the unresolved activation condition rather than infer it.

## Activation checklist

1. Locate the PPDU's approval act and its transitory provisions.
2. Verify the effective date specified by the instrument.
3. Verify inscription in the Registro Público de la Propiedad y del Comercio and record the evidence.
4. Confirm the exact territorial polygons covered by the PPDU.
5. Determine its hierarchy and interaction with PMDU, POEL, construction regulations, and other applicable instruments.
6. Identify transitional treatment for previously issued authorizations and pending applications.
7. Only then extract machine-readable zoning rules, each with an evidence reference to the source provision/map/table.
